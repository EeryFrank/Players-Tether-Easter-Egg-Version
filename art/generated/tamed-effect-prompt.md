## Deterministic finalization

The nontransparent source bounds were measured, a square source rectangle `(172, 176, 910, 910)` was selected, and it was downscaled with nearest-neighbour sampling into a `14x14` rectangle at `(1, 1)` on a transparent `16x16` canvas. No historical bone-texture pixels were used as an image input or reference.
